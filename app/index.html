Your road to Platinum

  1. Finish the story. Chapters 1–4 and the finale hand you 13 trophies and open the whole map.
  2. Clear the strands. Eight stranger strands (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone and friends) unlock a silver.
  3. Sweep the map. Explore all 52 areas, meditate at 18 spots and WAiNK It at 100 locations in one long tour.
  4. Grind the counters. 250 stunts, 50 challenges, 100 vehicles and 3 card sets — all repeatable in free roam.
  5. Hit 100%. The gold “A Long Road” falls when the Progress checklist is complete — then the platinum pops.

Nothing on the list is missable: every trophy stays earnable in post-story free roam. Estimated total: 60–80 hours.
